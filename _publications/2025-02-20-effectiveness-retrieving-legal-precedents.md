excerpt: 'We study how text summarization interacts with language model embeddings when retrieving legal precedents, aiming for cost-efficiency. Using decisions from a Brazilian administrative court, we compare a legal-domain Transformer with OpenAI embeddings. Full texts with long-context embeddings retrieve best, while summaries built from part-of-speech tags offer a strong trade-off between performance and cost.'
