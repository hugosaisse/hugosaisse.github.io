I co-founded ARTEH and led product and technology. After data ingestion, the platform shows climate-risk indicators and emission reduction targets, and produces a detailed report in minutes. It can support carbon certification applications and align corporate climate strategy with the TCFD recommendations. ARTEH received multiple Australian innovation awards.
