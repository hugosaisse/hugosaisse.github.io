title: "JurisMap"
excerpt: "An application that helps lawyers and judges find, compare and explore legal precedents with NLP, language models and visual analytics."
collection: portfolio
---

JurisMap came out of my Ph.D. research on making legal search more effective. It lets lawyers and judges find similar cases visually, compare a case against legal corpora, "talk to" jurisprudence, and draft legal documents with AI agents.

The evaluation code is public: [github.com/JurisMap/eval](https://github.com/JurisMap/eval).
