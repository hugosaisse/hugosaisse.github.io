---
title: "Zero-shot document classification for banking"
excerpt: "A configurable framework that classifies documents with large language models, without labeled training data."
collection: portfolio
---

This framework classifies documents from user-provided label descriptions, so technical and non-technical users can configure it without labeled examples. It was developed and validated on a real-world banking dataset.

The work is published in the International Journal of Data Science and Analytics: [read the paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41060-026-01077-x).
